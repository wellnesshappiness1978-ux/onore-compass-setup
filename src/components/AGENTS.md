# UI and feature rules
- Keep seven stable purpose IDs; phones expose five bottom tabs (Traits, Today, Consult, Art, Family) with Home and Settings in a fixed upper bar, while desktop retains all seven. Home resets scroll to zero; Family retains the business ID. Why: spacious mobile targets without losing features or legacy state.
- Torisetsu and compatibility are reference readings, not verified or paid entitlements. Why: do not overstate calculations.
- Consultation is session-only; owner or local preview roster targets omit missing fields and keep per-person histories separate. Browser speech and one 528Hz ambient piano instance must not overlap. Why: prevent invented context and audio collision.
- Keep nine-stars fixed grid illustrative and non-natal. Standard+ mandala is local SVG; VIP+ Cosmic Genesis is a local square Canvas framed as a golden compass-astrolabe with shell-growth relief confined to its outer metalwork, eight correctly ordered circular orbit bands at birth-instant heliocentric longitudes, visible birth-oriented Earth with its calculated birth Moon nearby, one fixed uploaded deep-space painting with personalized birth-color foreground details, a separate bottom linear Sun-plus-eight-planets physical-radius comparison, and a labelled separate-scale birthplace-facing Earth detail. Exports/shares remain labelled previews. Why: the personalized Solar System should feel collectible and unmistakably Agolmen while keeping orbit lines clear, separating readable planetary, lunar and true-size scales, and remaining private.
- My Page paid cards use seven-language plan copy and settings prices; clicks switch preview plans only on test hosts and production stays informational until billing exists. Active tier is status, not a sales card (use isUpgradePlan). Why: no false payment path.
- Appearance picker offers 15 cumulative tier-gated palette modes with shared-token specimens and locked previews; preserve mode IDs and the legacy session key, migrate palette choices to local persistence after hydration and never overwrite saved locked choices on membership changes. Why: unlock presentation options without changing user selections or entitlements.
- Share cards use the shared client canvas, localized readings, effective-plan palettes (Free main only), hidden names by default, and save fallback for native sharing. Why: protect identity and device compatibility.
- Family roster uses owner-only `team_members` rows when signed in and `onore-team` locally otherwise; team simulator uses deterministic pair scores and effective preview plan. Why: keep legacy storage compatible while guest preview grants no writes.
- Reuse HelioCalendar on Home/My Page and BirthColorGuide for color output rather than independent panels. Why: avoid tier-gate bypasses.
- Dashboard has one gesture-started ambient-piano player. Why: no overlapping audio.
- The ambient-piano player renders only inside Settings for VIP and above. Why: keep mobile workflows clear while preserving one gesture-started audio instance.
- Color details use three accessible steps and shared seven-language copy, with six illustrative roles and Free showing only its main color; no measured wavelength, therapeutic or family-root calculation claims. Why: distinguish educational color science from personalized symbolism.
- Appearance customization retains saved parts/background/icon attributes but exposes five cumulative plan-family thumbnails (minimal, celestial, sacred, harmony, sovereign) plus legacy palette swatches; unlocked family taps coordinate all parts and palette, locked taps only open isolated previews, and membership changes never alter the applied design. Shared NautilusIcon geometry feeds jewelry and AppearanceMotif backgrounds with bundled photographic artwork shared by specimens and the main canvas, while seven-language copy labels controls. Royal remains preview-only without an independent corporate contract. Why: allow mix-and-match without changing membership, astronomy or artwork exports.
- Consultation entry never auto-focuses the composer; explicit focus preserves page scroll and tab entry resets instantly after render. Why: avoid mobile keyboard/focus moving the page away from its top.

- Explanatory overlays use shared DialogContent and inline details use SacredDisclosure/Accordion. Browser-narration owns locale voice ranking, voice discovery, punctuation pauses and cancellation. Why: accessible detail surfaces and one device-native speech contract without external services.

- Today screen order (cheapest plan first, personal before family) and gates come from `todayFeatures` in plans.ts; locked cards show a one-line personal teaser plus a fictional sample dialog; share appears after the last unlocked card and at the bottom; each card ends with FeatureNotifySwitch (local, open-app-only reminders). Why: one source for order/gating and no false background-push promise.
