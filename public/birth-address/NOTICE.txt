Birth address reference data

International countries, first-level administrative areas and populated places:
GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/ , CC BY 4.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/
Files: countryInfo.txt, admin1CodesASCII.txt, cities500.zip
Retrieved 2026-10-22. Adapted to country JSON files; coordinates omitted,
place names deduplicated and grouped by first-level administrative area.
This reference gazetteer is not exhaustive or an official address registry.
Manual overrides are supported. Data is supplied without accuracy warranties.

Japanese municipalities use the existing Geolonia Japanese Addresses
representative-point dataset (CC BY 4.0):
https://github.com/geolonia/japanese-addresses
Japanese region IDs and existing saved profile text are preserved.
